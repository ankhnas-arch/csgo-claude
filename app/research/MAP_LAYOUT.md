# MAP_LAYOUT — compact Dust II-inspired layout (original, compressed)

Diagram: `research/map_overhead.svg` / `.png` (generated by `npx vite-node tools/map/diagram.ts` from `src/data/map/layout.ts`;
white = auto-generated walls, gold = jumpable ledges, brown = collidable props, dots = navigation cells, magenta = the four
reference cameras). North is up (−z). Scale ≈ 112 × 114 m playable bounds (real Dust II is roughly 1.6× larger per axis).

## What this is and is not
The reference pack (CS06–CS11) proves the *look* of five places, not the competitive topology. This layout is an **original
compressed plan** that preserves the relationships every Dust II player expects and is labelled as such in the credits and
scoreboard header. It is not a surveyed replica and makes no claim of official geometry.

## Sightlines and chokepoints (measured by walking; see `evidence/*/movement_routes`)
- Long: 60 m straight line from Long doors to A ramp (the longest sightline; AWP lane).
- Mid: T ramp to mid doors ≈ 60 m; doors leaves partially block the CT mid view.
- Chokepoints: Long doors (5 m wide, covered), mid doors (10 m opening with door leaves), B tunnel arch (8 m), CT ramp (8 m), catwalk stairs (6 m).
- Rotations (bots, measured in the integrated headless match): CT spawn → A ≈ 9 s, CT spawn → B ≈ 8 s, A ↔ B via CT spawn ≈ 17 s, T spawn → A via long ≈ 22 s, T spawn → B via tunnels ≈ 20 s (run speed 5.5–6.4 m/s).

## Navigation
The nav grid (1 m cells, 4 800+ walkable cells) is derived from the same rectangles and prop footprints as the collision, with
0.45 m clearance from walls/props; ramps are traversable when the neighbour floor differs by ≤ 0.55 m (step height) per cell.
Ledges (> 0.55 m and ≤ 2.2 m drops) create retaining walls that block walking but can be jumped from the low side.
